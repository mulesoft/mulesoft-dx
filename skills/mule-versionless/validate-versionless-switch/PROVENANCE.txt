mule-dx-platform-5.10.1-all.jar shades all four (verified: com/google/gson/Gson,

the bundle to platform's shading. If a future mule-dx-platform build stops shading any of
these, re-add that standalone jar. Dropping slf4j-api also removed the harmless startup
"StaticLoggerBinder" warning. Load-bearing jars, by contrast, fail fast if removed:
